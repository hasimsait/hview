## Calculation and sources

Use conservative manufacturer coverage endpoints, then add the requested reserve **once**, before rounding to packs:

- **Jotun:** area ÷ **9.6 m²/L per coat** × 2 × 1.15. The accessed TDS gives 9.6–13.4 m²/L per coat. Salon: **55.356 ÷ 9.6 × 2 × 1.15 = 13.26 L → one 15 L pail**.
- **Filli:** area × **0.080 L/m² per coat** × 2 × 1.15. Momento Silan gives 45–80 ml/m² per coat. Shared white rooms: **73.801 × 0.080 × 2 × 1.15 = 13.58 L → one 15 L pail**.

Primary references checked 26 October 2026:

- [Jotun 1876 WILD EARL](https://www.jotun.com/tr-tr/decorative/colours/interior/all-interior-colours/1876-wild-earl-interior), [specified Jotun coating](https://www.jotun.com/tr-tr/decorative/products/interior/all-interior-products/fenomastic-guzel-evim-zengin-mat), and [manufacturer TDS, code 42882, dated 14 September 2022](https://jotundatasheetsprod.blob.core.windows.net/tds/TDS%C2%A442882%C2%A4Fenomastic%20G%C3%BCzel%20Evim%20Zengin%20Mat%20Yeni%C2%A4tr-TR%C2%A4TR.pdf). A newer local label/TDS takes precedence over the accessed sheet.
- [Momento Silan TDS](https://www.filliboya.com/uploads/TDS_MOMENTOSILAN.pdf), [product and pack sizes](https://www.filliboya.com/urun/momento-silan).
- Filli colours: [KORAL 80](https://www.filliboya.com/renk/koral-80), [KAKTÜS 20](https://www.filliboya.com/renk/kaktus-20), [KEHRİBAR 50](https://www.filliboya.com/renk/kehribar-50), [KIRIK BEYAZ](https://www.filliboya.com/renk/kirik-beyaz).

The model has 2.7 m walls. Surface calculation uses wall centreline length × height, subtracts rectangular door/window openings, adds half-depth opening reveals per painted side, and adds selected painted column faces. Salon breakdown: **56.57 m² gross walls − 6.87 m² openings + 0.60 m² reveals + 5.04 m² column faces ≈ 55.36 m²**. Displayed terms are rounded.

This is a **conservative model estimate**, not a measured site bill: fixed wardrobes/cabinets and wall/column overlap are not deducted. Two finish coats assume suitably prepared and primed surfaces. Primer and ceilings are separate. Quantities are undiluted paint litres; thinning water does not reduce the amount to buy. No unsupported kg conversion is used. The 15% reserve is already included and should not be added again at checkout.
