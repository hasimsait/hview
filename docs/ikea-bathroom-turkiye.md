# IKEA Türkiye bathroom and shower references

265 individually referenced articles across 30 collections, checked on 15 October 2026. This adds 260 article numbers to the previous accessory catalogue and updates five matching entries with Turkish-market references, without duplicating their keys. The complete IKEA accessory catalogue now contains 396 articles, plus the existing five IKEA bookcases.

Names, functional descriptions, colours and filters are English. IKEA range names are retained. Every row links to an observed official IKEA Türkiye English product page and records its real article number. Some replacement editions have different article numbers but the same product name; the catalogue displays article codes to distinguish them. These references do not assert current stock availability.

The 265 articles include 66 towels and 199 other products. Colour, size and article editions count separately; this is a bathroom and shower selection, not a claim of 265 different shower hardware designs. Unrelated wardrobes, vanity combinations and search-result cross-sells were excluded.

## Planning models and dimensions

Models use bundled procedural geometry rather than manufacturer CAD or remote downloads. Caddies include open drainage slats or wire baskets, tiered and corner variants; shower fittings include spray heads, hoses, riser rails and overhead heads. Other models cover rails, swivelling arms, hooks, paper holders, shelves, curtain rods/rings, pleated fabric, bathroom sets, stools, laundry baskets and shelving. Cloth patterns are neutral visual cues rather than reproductions of IKEA artwork. The same catalogue and models are available in exported offline HTML.

Dimensions below are width × depth × height in centimetres. Published product dimensions are preserved where available. Missing extents, folded/hanging depths and installed projections are explicitly marked as estimates in the data and product cards. Package dimensions are not used as product dimensions. Rods start at the published minimum adjustable length; dimensions remain editable. Wall-mounted items, floor-standing items and countertop items use separate placement defaults.

The maintained inventory is `src/domain/catalog/IkeaBathroomProducts.json`; each row contains the source URL, English label, planning dimensions, dimension notes, placement and model specification.
