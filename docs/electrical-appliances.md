# Electrical fittings and household appliances

All catalogue names and descriptions are English. These are editable procedural planning models with local geometry, available in the full editor and offline exports.

## VIKO Karre

White single and double rocker switches, a single earthed socket and a double earthed socket. The models have square frames; socket models include recessed wells, open pin holes, grounding clips and centre screws. Switches start at 110 cm elevation and sockets at 30 cm. Frame and insert/control finishes can be changed independently.

References: [VIKO Karre sockets](https://viko.panasonic.com/en/products/switch-socket/karre/sockets), [VIKO Karre switches](https://viko.panasonic.com/en/products/switch-socket/karre/switches), [manufacturer catalogue](https://viko.panasonic.com/Uploads/File/panasonic-general-product-catalogue_1.pdf) and [double socket product dimensions](https://www.trendyol.com/en/viko/viko-karre-double-grounded-socket-90967x56-white-p-40496935). Double-socket face dimensions are 125.4 × 81.6 mm. Single faces use an approximate 82 mm square planning size. Visible projections are estimated; the concealed mechanism is excluded from the rendered depth.

## VRC Bubble

The supplied photos show glossy burgundy single and double circular rockers, plus an earthed socket with a cream outer frame and khaki insert. The models reproduce the convex annular frame, recessed socket insert, horizontal pin holes and upper/lower grounding clips. The double rocker has two separate semicircular controls.

The planning size is fixed at a circular 100 × 100 mm face and an estimated 25 mm visible projection. All three Bubble variants use this size when added, replaced or loaded from an existing legacy/canonical plan. Fixed dimensions prevent a rectangular outlet's size or concealed-box depth from stretching the model into an oval. The back of the fitting stays against the same wall face when its depth is corrected. Position, rotation and independent frame/control finishes remain editable. References are the supplied photographs and the [cream/khaki socket listing](https://www.trendyol.com/vrc-ticaret/ozel-tasarim-bubble-serisi-dis-krem-ic-haki-dekoratif-priz-a-kalite-plastik-mekanizma-dahildir-p-1164465592). Canonical retailer URLs retain their original language; no Turkish product labels are displayed in the application.

Electrical fittings use the existing Sockets display toggle. Selecting a newly added or replaced fitting enables this display automatically. The user can then hide it again with the toggle.

## Generic robot vacuum and air purifier

- **Robot Vacuum with Charging Dock**: 350 × 460 mm combined footprint, 150 mm dock height, low round vacuum body, perimeter bumper, navigation sensor and charging contacts. The robot and dock move together.
- **Cylindrical Air Purifier — White**: 300 mm diameter, 600 mm height, white casing, 576 visible intake perforations merged into one mesh, top outlet grille and control panel.

Both sizes are estimates. Existing `robot-cleaner` and `bactericidal_air_recirculator` items automatically resolve to the new models in legacy and canonical plans while retaining their IDs, positions and rotations. The exact historical 350 × 460 × 600 mm robot placeholder is corrected to the 150 mm combined height; deliberate custom sizes are preserved. Original working-plan files are left intact, and regenerated portable editors load the upgraded models.
