Material interpretation

Salon paint: Jotun 1876, base colour #D4CBB9. BILLY: oak carcass, shelves and backs; black wood-effect door frames and knobs; clear glass, including the extension units. The corrected set removes the metal-door placeholder and uses 55% viewing adaptation at 2700 K while retaining a warm-light appearance.

Capture-only lighting uses the saved lumen output, or an 806 lm reference where no lumen value was saved, with enabled fixtures at full comparison output. Fixtures above lowered ceilings are lowered for the capture. The unlit shower compartment receives a temporary 806 lm ceiling light. All overrides are recorded in the manifests; the uploaded plan is preserved byte for byte.
