# Historical decoration and palette proposals

**Superseded brief:** focus only on the salon, living/work room and master bedroom. Compare sofa fabric, independent BILLY body/door colours, bedroom accent paint, curtains and desk finishes. Bedding and sofa pillows are temporary styling props, not purchase decisions. Use the [active controlled study](../2026-10-30-focus/index.html).

These are proposals after the baseline render exercise. They do not alter the saved plan or the original/corrected HView views. Unbranded hex values below are planning colour references, not claimed manufacturer matches.

## Salon — keep Jotun 1876 fixed

The oak BILLY interiors and black door frames already work with the burgundy/cream upholstery and dark wenge furniture. The large empty wall and bare floor make the room feel harder than its material palette needs to be. I would start with textiles and one substantial artwork before changing furniture or adding more colours.

**A: retain blue and burgundy.** Keep the existing curtain colour. Add a pale ecru flatweave rug, roughly 160 × 230 cm, with the seating fronts on it where the actual clearances permit; use restrained charcoal linework rather than another large motif. One black-framed abstract in burgundy, cream and charcoal links the sofas to the black BILLY frames. Two small cushions are enough. Keep the central route clear.

**B: olive and burgundy.** Keep the exact same Jotun wall paint, sofas and oak/black bookcases. Change only curtains and small accents to muted olive `#6b7053`, with ecru `#dbd2c3` on the rug. This is a warmer, earthier direction; A retains the stronger cool/warm contrast. Compare the proposals under both temperatures before choosing a curtain fabric.

See [salon decoration comparison](concepts/salon-decoration-options.png). This uses one consistent viewpoint for the two proposals and both lamp temperatures; the full baseline includes both room viewpoints.

## Master bedroom — soften the colour blocks first

**A: terracotta and flax.** Retain accent wall `#764a40`, cream wardrobes `#dedbd1`, forest-green pillows and brass handles. Replace the large solid rust duvet block with flax bedding around `#d6c5b2`, retaining rust as a folded throw. This keeps the existing direction while reducing the amount of warm colour that 2700 K light amplifies.

**B: olive and ivory.** Try accent paint around `#59604c`, warm-ivory bedding `#e3d9c7`, the existing dark green pillows and a muted rust throw `#9a6551`. Keep the wardrobes and main pale walls unchanged. This is a deliberate alternative to the uploaded accent paint, not a correction of it.

In either case, keep additional furniture out of the wardrobe/bed passage. A small print and a narrow flatweave runner are more plausible than another floor-standing unit. The tall rectangle beside the entrance is a mirror, not an extra door.
