# Ivory & Taupe Striped Mosaic Backsplash

Available in **Tile & Marble**. Recreated from the supplied kitchen overview and close-up: small horizontal rectangles in ivory, cream, greige, taupe and muted brown, mixing plain glazed chips with fine horizontal stripes and narrow ivory joints.

Scale is estimated from the tape measure, not a manufacturer specification: approximately 25 × 12.5 mm per chip pitch, arranged as a seamless 30 × 30 cm repeat (12 columns × 24 rows). The texture includes the small internal joints. Sheet-boundary grout defaults to zero so repeated sheets do not acquire an extra border; the default layout is a straight grid. Gloss is represented by low material roughness.

The texture is embedded in standalone editor and viewer exports, including when the finish has not yet been used in the plan.
