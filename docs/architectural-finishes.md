# Architectural finishes

The **Architectural Finishes** catalogue category adds 24 generic, resizable assets across eight collections. The existing reference cornice/curtain pelmet is also shown here, retaining its original asset key and geometry for saved plans.

| Collection | Options | Default size, W × H × D (cm) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Skirting boards | Square-edge, chamfered, ogee, oak | 240 × 8–15 × 1.5–2.2 |
| Door architraves | Square-edge, stepped, fluted | 94 × 217 × 1.8–2.5 |
| Window sills | Painted board, bevelled stone | 120 × 3 × 25 |
| Thresholds | Oak saddle, metal transition, stone saddle | 90 × 0.5–2 × 4–15 |
| Ceiling cornices | Cove, stepped, ogee | 240 × 10–15 × 10–15 |

| Wall panelling | Shaker, oak slats with dark backing, beadboard | 60–120 × 100–240 × 1.8–2.5 |
| Decorative mouldings | Chair rail, picture rail, open wall frame | Rails 240 cm long; frame 70 × 100 × 1.8 |

All labels are English. Dimensions are adjustable planning references, not verified manufacturer specifications. Assets are built locally and are available in exported offline editors without network requests.

## Placement and editing

- Add through **Add Asset → Architectural Finishes**. Filter by collection using **Accessory type**.
- Wall pieces face into the room with their backs 1 mm off the wall. Skirting and wall panelling start at floor level; sills start at 87 cm, chair rails at 90 cm and picture rails at 210 cm. Adjust the elevation for the actual opening or room.
- Cornices and pelmets start immediately below the ceiling. Cornices sit against the wall. A pelmet projects in front of the curtain rail, leaving its underside and back open. The box option adds side returns.
- Dimensions describe the outside of an asset. Door surrounds are U-shaped, with no bottom rail or filled centre. Their standard 7 cm trim faces leave an 80 × 210 cm opening at the default size. The trim face width remains 7 cm when resized, subject to a proportional limit for very small surrounds.
- Panelling counts adapt to width; slats and acoustic-style backing have independent finish sections. Frames, rails, beads, sills and boards use the existing paint, wood, stone and other finish controls.
- Use separate straight lengths around corners and doorways. Position thresholds across the doorway, align architraves around an existing opening, and set sill widths to include the required overhang.
- These are independent catalogue objects: adding one does not replace automatic wall skirting, door frames, window curtains or the existing opening pelmets. Hide the corresponding existing finish or opening pelmet when using a custom piece in its place.
- Position, rotation, dimensions, visibility and section finishes persist through both plan formats and offline HTML export. Standard undo, duplication, hide and delete controls apply.

The geometry represents visible profiles, recesses and open spaces. Pieces do not automatically cut walls, snap to openings, create mitred room corners, follow later wall edits, or maintain ceiling contact after manual height changes; reposition them using the elevation control as needed.
